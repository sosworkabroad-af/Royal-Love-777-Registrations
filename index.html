    What happens after you register

    Royal Love families

    1. We welcome your family and confirm your book order.
    2. You begin the 7-day, 7-times-a-day devotional together.
    3. You are invited to the Love Audit and the Ambassador's Training Program.

    Churches

    1. We review your church and confirm your place among the 144 pilgrimage churches or the 288 Covenant Churches.
    2. We agree your delegation size and the pilgrimage you will attend.
    3. Your coordinator receives the programme, travel guidance and book orders for the delegation.

    3 January participants

    1. We confirm your registration and the number of people with you.
    2. You receive the programme and arrival details before the day.
